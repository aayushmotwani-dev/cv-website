02 / Profile

I started in electrical engineering at FH Aachen, completing a German-taught Bachelor’s degree focused on power systems, control engineering, and PLC automation. Taking five IEC 61131-3 projects independently from schematic design to commissioning taught me how industrial systems behave under real constraints — a habit I carried into a personal CODESYS project linking breaker interlocking to a Python control centre over IEC 60870-5-104.

During my Master’s in Computer & Media Engineering at RWTH Aachen, I moved from electrical hardware into production software and AI tooling. At Kellermann Lighting I built internal production apps — from a Python/Gradio BiRefNet cutout tool aligned with EU AI Act Article 50 to an offline German video pipeline (whisper.cpp, Ollama, Remotion) and the Kellermann Content Studio — alongside a remote Backend AI Engineering internship at FlyRank Corp. using Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, and Inngest.

Since October 2026 I have been an XR Development Intern at the Potsdam VR startup 2Sync, where I took “Take One” — a hands-first Quest 3 mixed-reality film studio with a 1:8 passthrough dollhouse, 1:1 room-scale scouting, body-recorded puppets, and in-headset AI video — from kick-off concept to the submitted Meta VR Start entry (v46), alongside trailer editing and a 27-build VR IMAX Theatre side project. I am now seeking a full-time engineering role in automation, software development, or XR — available four weeks after an offer and ready to relocate anywhere in Germany.
