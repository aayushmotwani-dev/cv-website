02 / Profile

Master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at RWTH Aachen, with a focus on immersive technologies, AI and machine learning, and audio signal processing. I hold a German-taught Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from FH Aachen, where I developed strong foundations in control engineering, measurement technology, automation, and technical problem-solving.

During my Bachelor’s studies, I gained hands-on experience in PLC-based automation and independently completed five automation projects, taking each one from schematic design through to commissioning. This strengthened my ability to understand technical systems, work methodically, and translate engineering concepts into functioning solutions.

Alongside my studies, I work as a working student in online marketing at Kellermann, where I contribute to social media content, product photography, and Shopware 6 development. This role allows me to combine technical thinking with creative execution, especially in digital content, product presentation, and web-based workflows.

I am particularly interested in roles where engineering, AI, immersive technologies, and digital media come together, and where I can apply both my technical background and creative skills to develop useful, engaging, and well-executed solutions.
